## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS built by `build.mjs` (Node) into `dist/`, one section per file, deploy GitHub + Vercel. Build-only dev dependencies (sharp, puppeteer-core). No runtime dependencies. This is the house default for client sites (see repo CLAUDE.md).

## Users

Farm managers, farm owners and agro-industry engineers in the west of Bahia and MATOPIBA (MA, TO, PI, BA) planning a large, expensive water structure: irrigation reservoir, canal, fish tank, settling pond. They decide six- and seven-figure works and need to trust the installer before calling. Most arrive on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Geomon Revestimento de Solo, a geomembrane installer based in Luís Eduardo Magalhães, BA, active since 2019. Success is a WhatsApp conversation started from the page, ideally already naming the service (each service has its own prefilled message).

## Positioning

The reservoir is only as strong as its weakest seam. Geomon tests every weld (double wedge weld, air test, destructive test on the tensiometer), its technicians hold the IAGI CWT certification, and the crew travels with its own mobile lodging trailer, so it works anywhere in MATOPIBA at the same standard.
